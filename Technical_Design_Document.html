1. Executive Summary & Economics

Company A is a high-volume biryani and regional Hyderabadi food chain operating 14 dine-in outlets and 3 cloud kitchens across Greater Hyderabad with 520 staff and ₹90–105 crore annual turnover. Currently, 55% of revenue (₹52.8 crore) flows through third-party food delivery aggregators charging 25–30% commissions. Furthermore, the brand loses 30 bulk catering orders every month due to manual phone ordering friction.

Verified Financial Model (Problem Statement Target):
Shifting just 12% of app orders to direct ordering saves 15 commission points (direct cost is 12%), yielding ₹95.04 lakh/year in net savings. Recovering 30 lost catering orders/month (₹18,000 ticket at 30% margin) adds ₹19.44 lakh/year. Combined annual bottom-line benefit equals ₹1.14 crore/year.

The Solution: A unified, end-to-end direct web platform featuring automated nearest-outlet georouting, a live Kitchen Display System (KDS) operating via WebSockets, central Menu CMS with outlet-level price overrides, instant 86-ing stock toggles, 5% direct rewards, and an outlet operations dashboard.

2. Architecture & Design Rationale

The system is engineered as a single deployable unit: an Express.js server that simultaneously serves REST API endpoints, hosts stateful bi-directional Socket.io WebSocket rooms, and delivers the compiled, optimized Vite + React 18 Single Page Application (SPA) on a unified single port (4000 locally, port-assigned on cloud hosts).

  • Zero Reviewer Setup Friction: Using Node 22+ native node:sqlite eliminates native C++ bindings (node-gyp, python build tools) and external database server daemons. A reviewer runs npm run install:all && npm run build && npm start and the entire product runs out of the box with zero external configuration.
  • Same-Origin Simplicity: Serving the Vite React bundle from Express eliminates CORS preflight delays and foreign-origin WebSocket handshake rejections in production environments.
  • Decoupled Pure Domain Logic: Core business rules (GST-inclusive calculations, coupon stacking, AI prep estimation, KDS ticket prioritization) are separated into pure, serverless modules covered by 25 unit tests with 100% pass rate in < 300ms.

4. Core User Journeys & Technical Flows

  • 1. Georouting & Outlet Assignment: Customer selects from 40+ curated Hyderabad localities (or clicks GPS "Locate Me"). Haversine distance is calculated against all 17 hubs; order auto-routes to the nearest hub within delivery radius.
  • 2. Food Menu & Customization: Single and Family (serves 4) portions with portion-specific pricing, add-ons (Extra raita, extra salan, extra papad), veg/non-veg indicator, spice level (Low/Medium/Hot), allergen tags, and time-based availability (Lunch 11:00–16:00 IST, Dinner 18:00+ IST, All Day).
  • 3. Checkout & Payment Simulation: Responsive slide-over cart drawer with 5% GST split, coupon discount (DIRECT10, WELCOME100), 5% direct loyalty point redemption, simulated UPI & Card payment (test decline for card ending 0000), DPDP Act 2023 marketing opt-in checkbox, completing in under 60 seconds.
  • 4. Live Kitchen Display System (KDS): KDS tickets update instantly via Socket.io WebSocket rooms (outlet:<id>). Displays elapsed timers, honest AI prep countdown, and color-coded late status. Kitchen staff progress orders with 1-tap: Placed → Preparing → Ready → Handed over. Overdue orders (10+ min late) trigger manager escalation.
  • 5. Instant 86-ing Stock Controls: Kitchen staff tap any dish button to toggle stock status. When marked 86'd, the dish vanishes in real-time from the customer menu for that outlet only.
  • 6. Party & Bulk Catering Request: Date picker enforces 48-hour advance notice server-side; guest counter calculates required food volume; catering manager reviews, confirms or declines with a custom customer note.
  • 7. Manager Operations Dashboard: Executive metrics showing total orders, gross revenue, median prep time (placed to ready), cancellations, and repeat customer ordering rates broken down per outlet.

5. AI & Automation Engine

Rather than unexplainable external LLM calls that fail during production rush hours, the AI prep-time engine is a transparent deterministic queue-load formula in server/src/prep.js: 

base      = slowest_item_prep + (extra_lines * 3 min) + (extra_units * 2 min)
queueLoad = min(active_outlet_orders * 4 min, 30 min)
total     = base + queueLoad
promised  = max(total, outlet_minimum_prep_minutes)

Where the AI can be wrong and built-in failure mitigations:

  1. Kitchen Rush Bottlenecks: Staff and managers have an inline override bar directly on KDS tickets (+15m, +30m, +45m, +60m or custom minutes) to immediately update promised time.
  2. Cold Start Floor: The outlet minimum prep floor (30–35 min) guarantees the system never under-promises during idle periods.
  3. Reorders: AI suggests reorders based on customer order history, but suggestions never auto-add to the cart.
  4. 10m+ Late Escalation: Any order 10 minutes past promise triggers an alert banner on KDS and updates customer with "Running late — kitchen is expediting".

6. UI / UX Design Decisions

  • Restrained Artisanal Palette: Grounded in deep charcoal (#111111), artisanal warm linen (#FAF8F4), hairline borders (#2B2823), and warm amber accents (#B45309). Zero emojis across the entire UI for a sleek enterprise look.
  • Strict 0px Border Radius (--r: 0px !important): Clean architectural square aesthetic reflecting modern brutalist luxury design.
  • 2-Metre KDS Readability: High-contrast dark theme (#141311) with stark white action buttons, huge quantities (×2), and color-coded late status designed for hot, noisy kitchen tablets.
  • Fluid 0.22s Physics: Silky smooth upward glide (translateY(8px) → 0) on view changes, side-drawer slide-ins, and zero horizontal tucking on mobile sticky cart.

8. Labelled Assumptions

  • [A1] Fictional Brand: Brand "Dum House", outlet addresses, FSSAI numbers, and phone numbers are formatted realistically for Hyderabad.
  • [A2] Price by City: All 17 hubs are within Hyderabad; "price by city" is implemented as one central Hyderabad base price plus per-outlet price customization overrides.
  • [A3] Meal Windows: Lunch is 11:00–16:00 IST; Dinner is 18:00–24:00 IST; All-day items remain available continuously.
  • [A4] Geodistances: Distances are calculated via standard Haversine formula against hub coordinates.
  • [A5] Manager Role: The "manager" role intentionally covers both the outlet manager and central marketing/admin team functions.
  • [A6] Sample Seed Orders: Seed data strictly matches the brief's sample table (WEB-778120 ₹1,180 Preparing; WEB-778131 ₹640 Ready; WEB-778146 ₹1,050 Late by 4m).
